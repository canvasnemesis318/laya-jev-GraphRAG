## ✨ What Is This?

laya-jev-GraphRAG is a friendly application that helps your computer find answers from your data using smart "brain" technology. Think of it as a super-powered search assistant that can understand connections between pieces of information, just like how you connect dots in your mind.

It works with your existing data storage systems and uses two types of smart models:
- **Laya**: Works locally on your computer (fast and private)
- **Jev**: Works through the cloud (more powerful for complex tasks)

You can switch between them anytime, like choosing between a quick coffee shop or a full-service restaurant.

## 🎯 How It Works (Simple Explanation)

Imagine you have a giant puzzle with many pieces. GraphRAG:

1. **Collects** – Gathers all your puzzle pieces (data).
2. **Organizes** – Puts related pieces together and shows how they connect.
3. **Understands** – Learns what each piece means and how pieces relate.
4. **Answers** – Uses this understanding to answer your questions with confidence.

All of this happens automatically in the background. You just see the results!
